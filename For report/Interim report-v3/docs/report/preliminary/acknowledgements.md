## Acknowledgements {#acknowledgements}

Our team of four Mechanical Engineering students and one Electrical Engineering student was pushed beyond its comfort zone to apply knowledge from our NUS coursework to the technically demanding construction of our Innovation and Design Capstone, the FOD Hunter. With the power of friendship, grit and determination, we were able to overcome the challenges faced in this semester and produce a critical function prototype of the FOD Hunter.

We would like to thank the following individuals:

Dr Mark De Lessio and Dr Tang Kok Zuea, for their advice and direction throughout the project. Their liaison with stakeholders kept communication channels clear. They also initiated discussions on a potential Memorandum of Understanding between the Republic of Singapore Air Force and NUS iDP, which would allow future projects like ours to be channelled towards real-world impact.

Ms Annie Tan, Mr Patrick Tan, Mr Alvin Pon and Ms Mei Yin, for their enthusiasm, support and guidance during our hands-on work in the electronics workshop.
The National Servicemen who shared both positive and negative experiences with us. Their insights shaped our design around their operational needs.
Ms Cindy Koh, Representative from the Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore (CAAS), for her insights on the aviation industry, which informed the constraints of our design.
