# Semester 2 plan — Takeuchi Asuka

<!-- Edit your plan between the region markers; the Overview and the Meet the Team page show this text. -->
<!-- #region plan -->

- Redesign the double wishbone suspension for CNC machining, with suitable bearings and rubber dampers to limit vibration transfer to the chassis, then verify the transfer function with a frequency response analysis.
- Redesign the FOD remediation system as two tools: one dedicated to small inanimate objects such as screws, bolts and small stones, and one for bigger, bulkier items up to the size of a bird carcass.
- Finalise both designs in reading week, iterate on them in vacation weeks 1 and 2, then fabricate them in vacation weeks 3 and 4 while waiting for components to arrive.
- In Semester 2, test the tools and the final suspension in week 1, integrate them with FOD Hunter in weeks 2 to 4, and gather user feedback and validate in weeks 5 and 6, with a site visit in recess week if possible. Test data is analysed in week 7 and documented in weeks 8 to 10, ahead of the report in week 12 and the presentation in week 13.
