### List of requirements {#sec-requirements}

FOD Hunter

Communication & Navigation

**Communication**

- Airfield-scale connectivity
- Singapore IMDA SRD regulatory compliance
- Non-internet link
- Asynchronous telemetry and waypoint streaming

**Navigation**

- Autonomous waypoint tracking
- Dynamic obstacle avoidance
- Geodetic to local coordinate frame transformation
- Sensor fusion to handle wheel slip

Mechanical

**Chassis, body and steering**

- House all electronics, vacuum and collection payloads
- Maintain ground clearance
- Zero mechanical fatigue
- Agile heading alignment over small targets

**Suspension**

- Natural frequency &lt; 10 Hz
- Static sag of ½ stroke length

**FOD remediation**

- Able to pick up FOD of varying size and weight, from screws up to a bird (3 cm to 18 cm, up to 300 g)

FOD Detection

**FOD detection subsystem**

- Detect FOD from 3 cm to 18 cm
- Latency: to be confirmed (fps)

Electrical & Power

**Microcontroller**

- Sufficient ports

**Computer**

- Radio frequency has to be approved by the RSAF

**Battery management system**

- 20 Ah
