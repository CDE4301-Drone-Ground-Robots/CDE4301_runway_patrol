CDE4301 Capstone • NUS × RSAF × RAiD • Phase 1

Development of an small-size autonomous wheeled robot for runway inspections

A high-speed collaborative multi-agent robotic system pairing autonomous UAV aerial macro-scouting with precision ground robotic extraction to safeguard active airfields.

Executive summary

This project addresses the manual, labour-intensive process of clearing Foreign Object Debris (FOD) from airport runways. The team was first introduced to the problem by industry partner Mr Harry Lim of IO Inspire, and subsequent stakeholder interviews highlighted the potential for an autonomous, multi-robot solution. Such a system may offer a technically feasible and commercially viable alternative, particularly for low- to medium-traffic airports locally and globally.

FOD Hunter is the remediation half of the two-robot FOD Hunter × FOD Argus system: FOD Argus detects debris, whilst FOD Hunter removes it. A critical-function prototype of FOD Hunter has been achieved, with clearly defined roles across eight subsystems: navigation, communication, mechanical (chassis, suspension and FOD remediation), electrical (power and microcontroller), and computer vision.

This report details the testing and outcomes of each subsystem, followed by future work and a Gantt chart for AY2026/27 Semesters 1 and 2.

Communication has been established with the Republic of Singapore Air Force as a potential end user, and a site visit for live testing of both FOD Hunter and FOD Argus is being pursued.
