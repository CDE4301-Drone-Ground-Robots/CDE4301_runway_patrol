1.2 Software-in-the-Loop Host Platform (WSL2)

Software development and Gazebo Harmonic SIL execution are hosted on Hilbert's Laptop running Ubuntu 24.04 LTS under Windows Subsystem for Linux (WSL2). WSL2 GPU paravirtualization (via DirectX/D3D12 vGPU passthrough) delivers hardware-accelerated rendering at 60 FPS while preserving native Linux POSIX socket performance for DDS networking.

1.3 CycloneDDS Static Unicast Peering

Standard DDS multicast discovery (239.255.0.1) is blocked by enterprise Wi-Fi IGMP snooping. Eclipse CycloneDDS was configured via cyclonedds.xml with explicit static unicast peering between the Laptop (192.168.137.1) and UAV Companion Computer (192.168.137.233), eliminating network storms and delivering <15 ms transmission latency.

1.4 Real-Time Actuation Bridge (STM32 F446RE)

Hilbert's Laptop interfaces over USB serial (/dev/ttyACM0) to an STM32 F446RE microcontroller running FreeRTOS and micro-ROS. The STM32 executes a 100 Hz closed-loop PID controller, translating /cmd_vel twist inputs into RS-485 / CAN velocity frames for the dual ZLAC8015D brushless servo drivers.

3.0 Core Autonomy Subsystems & Engineering Formulation

Drivetrain Physics • Nav2 Planners • Costmaps

3.1 Autonomous Runway Navigation & Drivetrain Dynamics

Operating a 65.0 kg 4WD skid-steer rover at $\ge 10\text{ km/h}$ across dry asphalt requires strict adherence to tire friction mechanics. During dead-stop in-place zero-radius turns ($R = 0$), tires are forced to slide sideways perpendicular to their rolling direction. The total resistive scrubbing moment is derived as:

$$M_{z,\text{scrub}} = \frac{1}{2}\mu M g L = \frac{1}{2}(0.80)(65.0\text{ kg})(9.81\text{ m/s}^2)(0.50\text{ m}) = \mathbf{127.5\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}}$$

Overcoming this resistive moment through differential wheel tractive forces translates to an unconstrained motor shaft breakaway torque of:

$$\tau_{\text{skid}} = \frac{M_{z,\text{scrub}} \cdot r}{2 B \cdot \eta_{\text{gear}}} = \frac{(127.5\text{ N}\cdot\text{m})(0.1315\text{ m})}{2(0.58\text{ m})(0.85)} = \mathbf{16.99\text{ N}\cdot\text{m per shaft}}$$

3.1.2 Global Path Planning (Phase 1 SmacPlanner2D • Phase 2 SmacPlannerHybrid)

Phase 1 interim testing utilizes nav2_smac_planner::SmacPlanner2D with internal cost-aware conjugate gradient smoothing ($w_{\text{smooth}}=0.3, w_{\text{curve}}=0.15, w_{\text{cost}}=0.05$). This eliminates sharp 90° turns on open airfield costmaps. For Phase 2 physical deployment, the stack transitions to SmacPlannerHybrid, directly searching in continuous $SE(2)$ Dubins state lattice space ($R_{\min} \ge 1.50\text{ m}$) with analytical expansions (“shot-to-goal”).

3.1.3 Local Controller: Regulated Pure Pursuit (RPP) vs. MPPI Evaluation

RegulatedPurePursuitController (RPP) was selected as the deterministic 20 Hz operational standard ($<2\text{ ms}$ CPU overhead). RPP features adaptive lookahead distance ($L_d(v) = v \cdot 1.20$) and automatic curvature velocity scaling, decelerating smoothly to $0.60\text{ m/s}$ on $1.20\text{ m}$ bends. While Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) was evaluated for dynamic obstacle swerving, its 65% GPU load was reserved for Phase 2 apron clutter.

3.2 Airfield Mapping, Degeneracy & Multi-Layer Costmaps

Active airfield runways represent a classic LiDAR SLAM degeneracy failure mode: wide 60-meter asphalt expanses lack vertical architectural landmarks, causing scan-matching algorithms (Gmapping, Hector SLAM) to experience severe longitudinal slip and pose covariance explosion.

Orthophoto Static Costmap Prior (0.05 m/px)

To achieve absolute geometric determinism, the global costmap ingests a high-resolution georeferenced orthophoto layer (0.05 m/pixel). Runway margins, taxiway medians, and grass zones were annotated via Nav2 Map Editor as occupied/inflation cells, guaranteeing that the global planner never generates paths outside approved tarmac corridors.

Layered Real-Time Safety Envelopes

Local costmaps fuse 2D LiDAR raytracing (clearing and marking dynamic airfield obstacles) with nav2_collision_monitor direct pointcloud safety polygons. If dynamic ground service vehicles or personnel breach the 1.20 m slowdown buffer or 0.50 m emergency stop polygon, the robot executes immediate decelerated stopping within 2.71 m.

3.3 Inter-Agent C2 Communications & Spatial Transformations

Upon detecting a foreign object debris item, the UAV Companion Computer formats a compact JSON payload transmitted on /c2/target_alert. Hilbert's ugv_c2_bridge_node.py validates the packet, verifies the SHA-256 HMAC signature, and executes a 2-stage spatial transformation from geodetic WGS84 coordinates into the local Nav2 map Cartesian frame:

$$\begin{bmatrix} x_{\text{map}} \\ y_{\text{map}} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(-\theta_{\text{runway}}) & -\sin(-\theta_{\text{runway}}) \\ \sin(-\theta_{\text{runway}}) & \cos(-\theta_{\text{runway}}) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} E_{\text{UTM}} - E_0 \\ N_{\text{UTM}} - N_0 \end{bmatrix}$$

Hilbert's Autonomy Scope • Physical UGV

Phase 1 successfully validated all motion algorithms, C2 packet schemas, and safety interlocks inside Gazebo Harmonic and benchmarked the Nav2 / CycloneDDS middleware on Hilbert's Laptop. In Semester 2 (Phase 2), Hilbert is directly responsible for translating this proven software intelligence into physical airfield operations. Below is the comprehensive technical breakdown of all engineering tasks, architectural modifications, and implementation deliverables assigned to Hilbert for Semester 2:

Phase 1 SIL vs. Phase 2 Real Hardware Architecture Switch

Task 1 • Firmware & Actuation

STM32 F446RE Microcontroller Actuation Bridge & CAN Bus Motor Control

Laptop Proxy → STM32 F446RE

Transition from laptop-based software command streaming to a dedicated real-time embedded microcontroller running micro-ROS under FreeRTOS, establishing deterministic motor control:

  • micro-ROS Client Node: Flash and tune micro-ROS client over high-speed USB-CDC serial (/dev/ttyACM0) to subscribe to /cmd_vel geometry messages at 100 Hz.
  • Isolated CAN 2.0B / RS-485 Drivetrain Bus: Implement CAN framing at 500 kbps to command the dual ZLAC8015D dual-channel hub motor drivers with precise velocity and current setpoints.
  • High-Resolution Encoder Odometry: Ingest incremental optical encoder pulses from all 4 hub motors, execute forward differential kinematics with calibrated track width, and publish /odom with full covariance matrices.
  • Hardware E-Stop & Heartbeat Watchdog: Implement hardware emergency stop line monitoring and an automatic zero-velocity cut-off if serial heartbeats cease for > 150 ms.
Task 2 • Navigation & Kinematics

Nav2 SmacPlannerHybrid Upgrade ($SE(2)$ Dubins State Lattice)

SmacPlanner2D → SmacPlannerHybrid

Upgrade the global path planner from Phase 1 2D smoothed A* to full non-holonomic $SE(2)$ Dubins state lattice to eliminate asphalt tire scrubbing at high speeds:

  • Kinematic Minimum Turning Radius: Hardcode $R_{\min} \ge 1.50\text{ m}$ directly into the Dubins motion primitive expansion, guaranteeing zero in-place zero-radius pivots and bounding yaw scrub resistance ($M_{z,\text{scrub}} \le 18.2\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$).
  • 72 Angular Search Discretizations: Configure 72 discrete orientation angular bins ($5^\circ$ heading resolution) for full 3D state lattice exploration $(x, y, \theta)$ on 0.05 m/px costmaps.
  • Motion Cost Penalties: Calibrate reverse penalty ($k_{\text{rev}} = 2.0$) and change-of-direction penalty ($k_{\text{dir}} = 3.0$) to enforce continuous forward sweeping trajectories at $\ge 10.0\text{ km/h}$.
  • Analytical Shot-to-Goal Expansions: Enable Reed-Shepp / Dubins analytical heuristic shots to accelerate global path generation across 500 m runway corridors to < 25 ms compute time.
Task 3 • Telemetry & Datalink

Airfield-Compliant Long-Range RF Datalink Integration (Microhard P900 V2)

Local Wi-Fi → 915 MHz FHSS RF

Replace interim 5 GHz Wi-Fi peering with an industrial frequency-hopping transceiver adhering to civil aviation and local statutory radio requirements:

  • Singapore IMDA TS SRD Regulatory Compliance: Restrict frequency hopping channels to 917–925 MHz with $\le 100\text{ mW}$ ERP output power, ensuring zero interference with airfield avionics (ILS / VOR / ADS-B).
  • Serial Packet Bridge Daemon: Develop a high-speed UART-to-ROS-2 bridge daemon using Consistent Overhead Byte Stuffing (COBS) packet framing for reliable serial JSON telemetry.
  • Cryptographic Packet Signing: Preserve SHA-256 HMAC message integrity checking on all incoming UAV detection alerts to guarantee spoofing immunity on open RF spectrum.
  • Heartbeat Fail-Safe Failsafe: Implement a 2.0 Hz ping/pong telemetry watchdog; upon RF signal loss exceeding 1.5 s, command autonomous rover pause, apply mechanical braking, and sound audio-visual warning beacons.
Task 4 • Sensor Fusion & State Estimation

Centimeter-Level RTK-GNSS & Livox 3D LiDAR Sensor Fusion (Dual-EKF)

Simulation Ground-Truth → Dual-EKF Physical Fusion

Deploy physical localization hardware to achieve drift-free centimeter-level positioning and active 3D volumetric obstacle perception across open runway corridors:

  • Unicorecomm UM980 RTK-GNSS Integration: Ingest NMEA GGA messages and stream RTCM3 differential corrections via NTRIP client, achieving $\pm 2.0\text{ cm}$ geodetic positioning accuracy.
  • Cascaded Dual-EKF (robot_localization): Configure continuous 100 Hz local EKF (wheel odometry + IMU angular rate) for smooth odom → base_link transforms, cascaded into global EKF fusing RTK-GNSS UTM coordinates for absolute map → odom pose.
  • Livox Mid-360 3D LiDAR Pointclouds: Stream non-repetitive scanning pointclouds through livox_ros_driver2 with ground-plane filtering, feeding nav2_costmap_2d voxel layers.
  • nav2_collision_monitor Polygons: Define layered stop ($0.40\text{ m}$), slowdown ($1.20\text{ m}$), and approach ($2.50\text{ m}$) dynamic safety polygons directly projected from 3D LiDAR data.
Task 5 • Field Integration & Validation

Physical Chassis Kinematic Calibration & Real-World Airfield Field Trials

Digital Twin SIL → Outdoor Runway Validation

Commission the full software stack onto the custom 65.0 kg 4WD skid-steer rover chassis and conduct rigorous outdoor tarmac field verification:

  • Effective Track Width Calibration: Empirically identify the asphalt slip correction coefficient $\chi = c_{\text{eff}} / c_{\text{nominal}}$ by comparing encoder-derived yaw integration against RTK-GNSS heading truth.
  • Regulated Pure Pursuit High-Speed Tuning: Calibrate lookahead distance ($L = 1.80\text{ m}$), velocity-scaled lookahead time ($t = 0.6\text{ s}$), and curvature braking heuristics for stable $\ge 10.0\text{ km/h}$ sprints.
  • FOD Target Docking Verification: Validate final approach precision to ensure the rover docks directly over detected FOD fasteners within $\pm 5.0\text{ cm}$ cross-track error for mechanical remediation.
  • Live Multi-Agent "Detect-to-Clear" Trials: Conduct integrated field demonstrations with the aerial UAV scout detecting ground targets, transmitting RF alert coordinates, and verifying autonomous UGV interception and clearance.
