Vision Processing Hardware: Gabriel's Laptop & USB Camera

Computer vision and real-time YOLO object detection are executed on Gabriel's Laptop utilizing an optical USB web camera interface. This architecture is adopted due to capstone budget constraints precluding the purchase of a new NVIDIA Jetson platform, with all existing EDIC Jetson developer units currently committed to the CDE3301 module. High-throughput video streams into the laptop over a dedicated USB 3.2 connection to ensure low-latency frame acquisition and reliable real-time inference.
