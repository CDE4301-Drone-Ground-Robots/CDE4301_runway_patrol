🍂 Semester 1 Focus & Milestones (Aug – Dec 2026)
  • Problem & Requirements Definition (Wk1–Wk4): Primary & secondary airfield research, stakeholder operational needs.
  • Subsystem Calculations (Wk2–Wk5): Hub motor torque, vacuum CFM suction flow, MacPherson/trailing suspension geometry, power budget.
  • Critical Function Prototypes (CFP) (Wk6–Wk11): ROS 2 simulation & SLAM Toolbox mapping, YOLOv8 dataset annotations, vacuum hood fabrication.
  • ⭐ Interim Presentation & Documentation (Wk12–Wk13): Critical Function Prototype demonstration and interim report submission.
🌱 Semester 2 Focus & Milestones (Jan – Apr 2027)
  • Hardware Assembly & Wiring (Wk1–Wk4): Physical UGV chassis assembly, STM32 F446RE motor interfacing, PDB soldering.
  • Full Stack Integration (Wk3–Wk7): Nav2 autonomous navigation tuning on physical UGV, multi-agent UAV-UGV telemetry bridge.
  • Field Testing & Tuning (Wk7–Wk10): Airfield obstacle avoidance sweeps, runway expansion joint clearance trials at ≥10 km/h.
  • 🏆 Final Capstone Showcase (Wk11–Wk13): Final comprehensive documentation submission and live multi-agent operational demo.
