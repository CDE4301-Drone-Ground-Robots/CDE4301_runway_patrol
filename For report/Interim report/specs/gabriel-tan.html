Gabriel Tan — Computer Vision & Sensor Fusion

Gabriel Tan

Subsystem Scope: Computer Vision & Sensor Fusion

Matric: A0308452H

Gabriel Tan leads the Computer Vision and Sensor Fusion subsystem. Scope focuses on YOLO object detection model training, multi-modal perception combining camera streams with LiDAR point clouds, and target FOD classification.

🎯 Engineering Scope Objectives
  • YOLO Object Detection: Train and evaluate real-time YOLO object detection models on airfield Foreign Object Debris (FOD) datasets.
  • FOD Classification: Detect and classify 4 hazard categories: Metallic Fasteners, Asphalt Chips, Maintenance Tools, and Tire Rubber.
  • Sensor Fusion: Combine RGB-D depth camera spatial bounding boxes with 3D LiDAR point clouds for close-range verification.
  • Edge Acceleration: Optimize TensorRT hardware acceleration for real-time inference (>50 FPS on Jetson Orin AGX).
🛠️ Technical Execution & Responsibilities
  • Collected and annotated custom dataset of airfield debris objects under varying lighting conditions.
  • Benchmarked YOLOv8 vs YOLOv11 architectures (YOLOv11 Nano achieved 95.2% mAP@50 at 52 FPS).
  • Developed ROS 2 vision processing node subscribing to image topics and publishing 3D target coordinates.
  • Integrated spatial depth filtering to eliminate shadows and pavement painted line false positives.
