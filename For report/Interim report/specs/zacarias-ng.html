Zacarias Ng

Subsystem Scope: Chassis Design & Kinematics Calculations

Matric: A0306899H

Zacarias Ng is responsible for the overall UGV chassis structural frame sizing, differential drive kinematics mathematical modeling, wheel odometry equations, and IP65 weatherproof enclosure design.

🎯 Engineering Scope Objectives
  • Differential Drive Kinematics: Derive forward and inverse kinematics matrix equations for 4WD differential drive velocity control.
  • Chassis Frame Sizing: Calculate center-of-mass dynamics, slope stability, and ground clearance optimization.
  • IP65 Weatherproof Enclosure: Design dust-tight and rain-resistant housing for onboard compute and motor controllers.
  • Wheel Odometry Dynamics: Formulate encoder pulse-to-distance mathematical transformation equations for navigation.
🛠️ Technical Execution & Responsibilities
  • Calculated differential drive kinematics matrices linking wheel angular velocity to linear robot velocity (v, w).
  • Designed structural aluminum extrusion layout, motor mounting plates, and wheel hubs.
  • Engineered IP65 sealed electronics enclosure with heat-sink ventilation passages.
  • Validated chassis ground clearance dynamics across curb obstacles and uneven pavement.
