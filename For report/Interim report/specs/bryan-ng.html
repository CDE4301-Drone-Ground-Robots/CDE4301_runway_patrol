Bryan Ng

Subsystem Scope: Electrical System, Charging Dock & STM32 Firmware

Matric: [ADD ID]

Bryan Ng leads the electrical system design, power distribution network, autonomous contact-charging dock interface, and low-level embedded STM32 microcontroller C/C++ firmware programming.

🎯 Engineering Scope Objectives
  • Power Distribution Network: Design 48V 20Ah LiFePO4 battery power architecture with 100A BMS, 100A kill switch, and multi-rail DC buck converters (20V for Hilbert's Laptop, 12V auxiliary, 5V logic).
  • STM32 Firmware Programming: Write deterministic embedded C/C++ firmware on STM32 for closed-loop CAN bus communication with ZLAC8015D dual servo driver.
  • FOD Actuator & Power Circuit: Build safe switched high-current circuit for 2800W vacuum via 50A ANM fuses and solid state relays.
  • Safety & Diagnostics: Build 100A master kill switch, RF wireless remote ESC shutoff, and 3-tier indicator light tower.
🛠️ Technical Execution & Responsibilities
  • Created master system electrical schematic diagram and power consumption load budget for 48V nominal bus.
  • Engineered 20V buck conversion rail specifically dedicated to powering Hilbert's Laptop (onboard UGV computer) due to budget limits and CDE3301 EDIC Jetson allocation.
  • Integrated XT60 1-to-6 and XT60-to-XT30 power distribution boards with 201 ANM fuse box and blade fuse blocks.
  • Programmed STM32 microcontroller with micro-ROS for real-time CAN bus telemetry and motor velocity feedback.
