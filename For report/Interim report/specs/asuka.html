Asuka — FOD Pick Up & Suspensions (Mechanical)

Asuka

Subsystem Scope: FOD Pick Up & Suspensions (Mechanical)

Matric: A0308285Y

Asuka leads the mechanical development of the physical FOD remediation pickup mechanism and the independent chassis suspension system. Scope focuses on SolidWorks CAD modeling, intake vacuum throat optimization, structural FEA bump impact analysis, and terrain travel compliance.

🎯 Engineering Scope Objectives
  • FOD Remediation Pickup: Design and build high-efficiency vacuum suction intake hood capable of extracting metal, stone, and rubber debris.
  • Suspension Damping: Engineer independent suspension geometry to isolate electronic payloads from runway surface vibration.
  • Debris Containment Box: Build quick-swap sealed 5-liter collection bin with magnetic quick-release latch.
  • Structural Integrity: Perform SolidWorks FEA stress analysis under 3G vertical bump impact loads.
🛠️ Technical Execution & Responsibilities
  • Created 3D SolidWorks CAD assemblies for chassis frame, intake throat, and cyclone separation box.
  • Optimized 15° intake nozzle geometry for high-velocity suction airflow (135 CFM @ 24V).
  • Fabricated aluminum 6061-T6 structural mounts paired with carbon-fiber reinforced PETG air ducts.
  • Evaluated suspension compliance and dampening performance across rough tarmac surfaces.
