⚠️ Operational Threat & Problem Definition
📖 Read Full Introduction Spec

Airfield operational safety depends directly on maintaining clean runway surfaces. Foreign Object Debris (FOD)—encompassing loose jet engine fasteners, asphalt chips, tool components, and tire rubber fragments—poses a severe threat to military and commercial aviation worldwide.

💥 Engine Ingestion Risk

Small metallic particles or fasteners ingested into high-speed turbine blades cause catastrophic compressor blade destruction and engine failure during takeoff.

🛞 High-Speed Punctures

Sharp metallic fragments cause high-velocity tire blowouts during 250+ km/h rollout phases, compromising directional control and stability.

⏱️ Sortie Rate Compromise

Manual sweep closures restrict airbase runway availability, causing flight delays and restricting emergency military sortie generation.

Key Research & Engineering Gap: Existing airside UGVs fall into two extremes: either heavy, costly diagnostic inspection rovers (Airtrek, Roboxi) that only log coordinates without physical retrieval, or civil engineering machines (Guimu) built exclusively for sub-surface radar. Runway Patrol uniquely couples high-speed aerial cueing (UAV) with targeted, active physical extraction (UGV) to achieve true closed-loop "Find-and-Fix" airside remediation.
